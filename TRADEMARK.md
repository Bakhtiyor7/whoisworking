# Name and icon

Who's Working is open source under the [GNU General Public License, version 3](LICENSE). That licence covers the code. It does not cover the project's name or its icon.

## What you may do

- Use, study, change and share the code under the terms of the licence.
- Say that your project is based on Who's Working, or works with it, and link here.
- Use the name and icon to refer to this project, for example in an article or a list of apps.

## What we ask of a fork

If you distribute a changed version, give it another name and another icon. "Who's Working" and the app icon (`Resources/WhoIsWorking.icns`) are how people recognise the builds published from this repository, and a fork that used them would be taken for one of those builds.

A build you make for yourself can keep both.
